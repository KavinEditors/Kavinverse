NEXA

NEXA (Next-gen Executive Assistant) is an advanced voice-controlled AI designed to streamline productivity through real-time automation. Powered by Groq LLM intelligence, it understands natural speech and performs complex tasks instantly. It integrates speech recognition, voice responses, and Windows automation for a seamless hands-free experience. NEXA can open apps, search the web, manage files, control system functions, and execute workflows intelligently. A floating visual UI indicator keeps NEXA always accessible without interrupting your focus. Built for creators, students, and professionals — NEXA transforms everyday actions into efficient intelligent automation.
