Luna

Luna is an intelligent extension for browsers powered by advanced AI models that assists users directly within their browser environment. It can analyse complex webpages, summarize long PDF documents instantly, extract key insights, and answer contextual questions about the content currently being viewed. The goal of Luna is to transform passive browsing into an interactive and productive experience.

Project WATER

Project WATER is an IoT-driven initiative focused on improving global access to clean and sustainable water resources. The system integrates smart sensors, monitoring stations, and automated recycling pipelines to track water quality, detect contamination early, and manage distribution intelligently. This project aims to provide communities with reliable water systems through real-time data and efficient infrastructure.
